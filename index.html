Our Story

10+

Years of baking

Baked with obsession,
not a factory.

Maharashtra Bakery started in a Portland kitchen in 2015 when founder Priya Mehta couldn't find a cookie that matched what she remembered from her grandmother's kitchen. So she made one. Then another. Then a hundred more.

Today every batch is still mixed by hand in small quantities — never more than 48 cookies at a time — because that's the only way to get the caramelization right on every single edge.
